# IS4FIRES v2.0 (FMI Wildfire Emission & Smoke Forecast System)

## What this model is
**IS4FIRES** (Integrated System for wild-land Fires), version 2.0, is FMI's global wildfire smoke system. It is really two coupled products distributed together:

1. A **fire-emission system** that converts satellite fire-radiative-power (FRP) observations into gridded particulate-matter emission fluxes; and
2. A **global smoke forecast / dispersion** run — a SILAM simulation that transports those fire emissions to produce near-real-time and forecast fields of smoke PM concentration and smoke aerosol optical depth.

IS4FIRES is the fire-emission engine behind the wildfire PM tracers seen in FMI's SILAM air-quality output (the `cnc_PM_FRP` / `cnc_PM_GFAS_*` fields in [SILAM Global](./silam-global.md) and the regional SILAM entries). It is distributed on the same FMI THREDDS server. Note it is generated by an **older SILAM core (v5.8)** than the v6.1 AQ forecasts.

Primary intended use: wildfire smoke monitoring and forecasting, and provision of fire emissions to chemistry-transport models.

## What area it covers
- **Coverage:** Global
- **Domain details:** Regular global latitude–longitude grid, **3600 × 1780**, **0.1° × 0.1°** (~10 km), ~89°S–89°N

---

## Basic details
- **Model type:** Wildfire emission model + global smoke dispersion / forecast
- **Model system / core:** SILAM **v5.8** (source attribute `silam_v5_8 SVN (r603182)`, verified July 2026); IS4FIRES emission processor v2.0
- **Horizontal resolution:** 0.1° (~10 km) global
- **Vertical levels:** Emissions are surface source terms; the smoke concentration field (`cnc_PM_FRP`) is distributed as an air concentration — *confirm surface vs. multi-level before documenting as 3D*
- **Forecast length:** 120 h (5 days) for the forecast collection
- **Update frequency / cycles:** 1× daily
- **Temporal output resolution:** Hourly (smoke forecast/dispersion); 3-hourly (emission fluxes)

---

## Meteorological driver
- **Driving NWP model:** ECMWF IFS (standard SILAM forcing, consistent with FMI's global SILAM) — *verify*
- **Coupling:** Offline (one-way); the smoke dispersion is a SILAM run driven by IFS meteorology

---

## Emissions (the core of IS4FIRES)
- **Wildfire emissions:** Derived from **satellite fire-radiative-power (FRP)** observations (MODIS; VIIRS incorporated in v2.0) — *verify exact sensor set for v2.0*. FRP is converted to primary-PM emission fluxes.
- **Emitted species:** Fire primary particulate matter (`PM_FRP`), distributed across several size modes (`ems_PM_FRP_m_17`, `ems_PM_FRP_m1_1`, `ems_PM_FRP_m3_1`); units are emission intensity (mass s⁻¹)
- **Not included:** Anthropogenic, biogenic, dust, or sea-salt emissions are out of scope — IS4FIRES is a dedicated fire-emission system

---

## Chemistry and aerosols
- **Treatment:** Transport of primary fire-emitted PM (smoke); no gas-phase chemistry. Smoke is carried as a size-resolved primary aerosol (`PM_FRP`). Secondary chemistry, if any, is not part of this product — *verify*

---

## What it provides
Three product families, all global 0.1°:
- **Fire emissions** (`ems_PM_FRP_*`): 3-hourly gridded PM emission fluxes in several size modes (source terms for CTMs)
- **Smoke forecast** (`IS4FIRES-fc`): hourly **smoke PM concentration** (`cnc_PM_FRP`, kg m⁻³) and **smoke aerosol optical depth at 550 nm** (`ocd_PM_FRP`, dimensionless), out to 120 h
- **Smoke dispersion archive** (`IS4FIRES-disp`): the same concentration/optical-depth fields as a multi-year hourly archive (~8+ years, extending to the present)

## Notes
- **Two products in one system.** The emission fluxes are *input data* for chemistry-transport models; the forecast/dispersion collection is a standalone **global smoke forecast** (functionally comparable to NOAA HRRR-Smoke or GEOS fire-aerosol products, but global at 0.1°).
- **Feeds SILAM.** The `PM_FRP` (and GFAS) smoke tracers in FMI's SILAM AQ forecasts originate here — cross-reference [SILAM Global](./silam-global.md) and the regional SILAM entries.
- **Older core.** Distributed output is generated by SILAM v5.8, not the v6.1 used for the current AQ forecasts.
- **Placement decision.** Filed here alongside the global SILAM AQ entry as a global, FMI-operated, AQ-relevant smoke product; alternatively it could sit in a dedicated fire/smoke section if one is established.
- Emission-flux datasets are arguably ancillary (source terms) rather than a forecast product — if the catalogue scope is forecast-only, the emissions collections could be mentioned rather than fully catalogued, with the `IS4FIRES-fc` smoke forecast as the primary entry.
