# ICON-ART-EU-EPS (European Mineral Dust Ensemble)

## What this model is
ICON-ART-EU-EPS is the 10-member ensemble configuration of DWD's regional [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md) mineral-dust forecast. It runs the ICON dynamical core with the online-coupled ART (Aerosols and Reactive Trace gases) module on a **dedicated regional grid built specifically for prognostic aerosol forecasting** — ~10 km since 6 October 2026, ~13 km before — and provides probabilistic guidance on mineral-dust emission, transport, sedimentation, and deposition — chiefly Saharan dust transported from North Africa into Europe — alongside a standard meteorological field set.

It was published on the DWD Open Data Server in **April 2025** together with the deterministic ICON-ART-EU, the global ICON-ART, and ICON-ART-EPS, on the `/weather/nwp/v1/m/` tier that uses DWD's revised URL scheme.

> **The domain is not the ICON-EU nest.** ICON-ART-EU-EPS runs on DWD grid **#61** (~10 km) since 6 October 2026, and before that on grid **#51**, described in DWD's grid catalogue as a *"Regional R03B07 grid (Europe, North Atlantic and North Africa) … Bigger nest for prognostic aerosol forecasts with ICON-ART, 13 km resolution."* DWD's 6 October 2026 change notice calls it the ICON-ART "large nested domain covering Europe, North Africa and the Northern Atlantic (ICON-ART-EU-NA²)", nested in the global [ICON-ART](../../global/germany/icon-art.md) / [ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md) domain (grid #39 since 6 October, #36 before). This is a purpose-built aerosol domain reaching the equator and 82°N, not the operational ICON-EU nest. See *What area it covers*.

> **This entry is filed under `air_quality_models/` rather than `ensemble_models/`**, following the repository's convention that phenomenon ensembles use their phenomenon template. It uses `air-quality-model.template.md` with the optional **Ensemble configuration** section retained. Cross-linked in both directions with [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md).

## What area it covers
- **Coverage:** Europe, the North Atlantic, and North Africa — DWD refers to this domain as **ICON-EU NA²**
- **Domain bounds (verified from the distributed `CLAT` / `CLON` fields, 2026-10-18 12 UTC run):** approximately **0.20°S – 82.18°N, 70.55°W – 70.55°E** (cell centres). The southern edge reaches the equator, putting the full Saharan dust source region inside the domain rather than at its boundary; the northern edge reaches the high Arctic. The domain was not changed on 6 October 2026 — DWD's notice says ICON-ART "already has a much larger nested domain" and is not affected by that day's ICON-EU enlargement — so the small differences from the grid #51 values (0.27°S – 82.26°N, 70.62°W – 70.62°E) come from the finer cell spacing at the edges.
- **Native grid (since 6 October 2026, verified):** DWD grid **#61** — **1,013,404 cells**, `numberOfGridUsed = 61`, `uuidOfHGrid = 7fe5661c7b47abbc0766699947a72c40`, `gridDefinitionTemplateNumber = 101`, `gridType = unstructured_grid`. ~10 km nominal spacing. DWD's 28 July 2026 newsletter gives 13 km → 10 km and 1.78× the grid points; 1,013,404 / 571,088 = 1.77.
- **Until 5 October 2026:** R3B07 regional mesh, DWD grid #51 — 571,088 cells, ~13 km.
- **Grid lineage:** Grid #51 was generated by J. Foerstner, 2022-02-28, with grid #36 (the then global ICON-ART / ICON-ART-EPS mesh) as parent; its 20 km predecessor was grid #50 (R2B07, same domain, 2021-10-26). The catalogue entry for grid #61 could not be checked for this update (the MPI-M server was not reachable from the verification environment), so its formal parent and generation date are not stated here; grid #39 is the natural assumption but is unconfirmed.
- **Not the ICON-EU-EPS grid.** Since 6 October 2026 both this system and [ICON-EU-EPS](../../../ensemble_models/regional/de/icon-eu-eps.md) are nominally 10 km, but ICON-EU-EPS uses grid #63 (342,428 cells) over the smaller ICON-EU domain. The cells do not correspond.

### Georeferencing
GRIB2 messages carry **no lat/lon**. Two working routes:

1. **Use the `CLAT` and `CLON` parameter folders** on this feed (plus `ELAT`/`ELON` for cell vertices). DWD publishes cell coordinates as GRIB fields (one value per cell, step 0 only) precisely as a lightweight alternative to the grid file. They changed with the grid on 6 October 2026 — re-download them rather than reusing a grid #51 copy.
2. **Fetch the grid file from MPI-M** via `https://icon-downloads.mpimet.mpg.de/dwd_grids.xml` — grid number **61** for current data, **51** (`icon_grid_0051_R03B07_N02.nc`) for data before 6 October 2026. Needed for cell connectivity, neighbour relations or remapping weights. DWD's regridding guide is at https://gitlab.opencode.de/dwd/open-data/regrid-native-icon-grid.

`https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/lib/cdo/` never held a file for either ART regional grid, and the whole directory is deleted after 30 November 2026 (DWD Open Data newsletter, 2 September 2026).

## Basic details
- **Model type:** Air quality / atmospheric composition (ensemble)
- **Model system / core:** ICON + ART module, online-coupled, nested configuration on grid #61 (grid #51 until 6 October 2026)
- **Coupling:** Online — dust and meteorology evolve together within a single integration, with dust–radiation and dust–cloud feedbacks acting on the forecast
- **Horizontal resolution:** ~10 km (grid #61) since 6 October 2026; ~13 km (grid #51) before. **Identical to the deterministic [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md)**, which was verified to use the same grid #61 and the same 1,013,404 cells — the ensemble is not coarsened. DWD's change notice confirms that ICON-ART's deterministic and ensemble forecasts run at the same resolution.
- **Vertical levels:** 74 model levels (verified from published level indices and the `HHL` header, `nlev = 75` — matching the ICON-EU-family vertical structure). Unchanged by the October upgrade. Only small subsets are distributed; see *What it provides*.
- **Forecast length (verified):** +120 h at **all four cycles**
- **Update frequency / cycles:** 4× daily (00, 06, 12, 18 UTC), with dust parameters present in **every** cycle
- **Temporal output resolution:** Parameter-dependent; thirteen distinct step schedules coexist in a single run. The main meteorological group is hourly to +75 h then 3-hourly; `TAOD_DUST` is hourly to +72 h then 3-hourly. See *What it provides*.
- **Observed publication latency (v1, measured 2026-10-18/19):** first files ~2 h 37–41 min after cycle time; run complete ~3 h 13–15 min. Example: 2026-10-19 00 UTC run 02:37:05 → 03:14:46 UTC; 2026-10-18 06 UTC run 08:39:02 → 09:13:08 UTC.

## Meteorological driver
- **Driving NWP model:** Self — ICON-ART-EU-EPS *is* ICON with ART run over the ICON-ART-EU-NA² domain (grid #61 since 6 October 2026, grid #51 before). There is no external meteorological driver.
- **Coupling:** Online (two-way between dust and meteorology)
- **Lateral boundary conditions:** Partly documented. DWD's 6 October 2026 change notice describes ICON-ART as a global domain plus this "large nested domain", with the deterministic forecast and 10 ensemble members run at the same resolution, so the global ART ensemble is the boundary source — but DWD does not state whether the two run as a genuine two-way nest within one integration or as a one-way-forced limited-area run, nor whether member *n* of this ensemble is bounded by member *n* of [ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md). Grid parentage establishes mesh derivation, not runtime coupling. Worth confirming with DWD.

## Chemistry and aerosols
The ART configuration matches the deterministic [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md) and the global [ICON-ART](../../global/germany/icon-art.md); see those entries for the full description. Summarised here:

- **Scope:** Mineral dust only. Not a full air quality model — no ozone, no NOₓ, no PM speciation, no gas-phase chemistry in the operational configuration, and **no pollen** (see [ICON-ART Pollen](./icon-art-pollen.md), a separate configuration on a separate channel).
- **Aerosol treatment:** Modal — three log-normal dust modes distributed as `DUSTA` / `DUSTB` / `DUSTC` (prognostic mass mixing ratios, kg kg⁻¹) plus `DUSTA0` / `DUSTB0` / `DUSTC0`. GRIB names identify these as fine, medium, and coarse modes.
- **Gas-phase chemical mechanism:** Not applicable to this configuration.
- **Optical properties:** Dust optical depth is diagnosed and distributed column-integrated (`TAOD_DUST`) and per model layer (folder `AER_DUST` — the GRIB `shortName` is **`AOD_DUST`**, *"Diagnostic mineral dust optical depth"*, which does not match the directory name; the same mismatch exists on the global feed).
- **Lidar-space diagnostics:** Attenuated backscatter at 532 nm and 1064 nm, in ground-looking (`CEIL_BSC_DUST`, ceilometer geometry) and space-looking (`SAT_BSC_DUST`, satellite geometry) forms. These are the only parameters here with a wavelength dimension in the URL path.

## Emissions
- **Dust scheme:** Online — emission computed within the run from the model's own surface state, diagnosed against a threshold friction velocity. Both `USTAR` (frictional velocity) and `USTAR_THRES` (threshold friction velocity) are published, making the emission trigger inspectable from the public data. With the Saharan source region fully inside this domain, emission here is genuinely internal rather than imported across a boundary.
- **Accumulated dust budget terms:** Per-mode accumulations of emission, dry deposition, wet deposition (convective and grid-scale, separately), and sedimentation — 15 parameters.
- **Anthropogenic / biogenic / wildfire emissions:** Not applicable — no species other than mineral dust are carried.
- **Whether emission parameters are perturbed across members:** TBD (see *Ensemble configuration*).

## Ensemble configuration

- **Ensemble type:** Perturbation-based — a single model configuration run as multiple perturbed members, not a multi-model ensemble.

- **Ensemble size:** **10 members, no control.** Verified from the feed (`e/01` … `e/10`, in all four cycles) and confirmed by DWD's April 2025 announcement, which states both ART ensembles consist of 10 ensemble runs. GRIB `perturbationNumber` runs 1–10 and matches the directory number exactly; `numberOfForecastsInEnsemble = 10`, consistent with what is actually published. No `e/00` and no `perturbationNumber = 0` exists. Consumers expecting a control/perturbed split need to adapt — this matches DWD practice across ICON-EPS, ICON-EU-EPS, ICON-D2-EPS, and the global ART ensemble.

- **Source(s) of perturbations:** **TBD — not documented by DWD.** Members are demonstrably distinct (`TAOD_DUST` at +12 h: member means 0.146, 0.125, 0.136, 0.153 for members 01, 02, 07, 10, with RMS differences of 0.042–0.049 against member 01), so meaningful spread exists. But neither the perturbation method, the member correspondence with [ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md), nor whether the ART side is perturbed at all — as opposed to all spread propagating from meteorological perturbations through the emission scheme — is stated in the announcement, the ICON documentation, or the GRIB headers. The same gap exists for the global ART ensemble; a single question to DWD would resolve both.

- **Chemistry / emissions differences vs deterministic sibling:** None identified. Same ART module, mode structure, and emission scheme; the differences are in what is published, not how the model is configured.

- **Resolution, species, and output differences vs deterministic sibling:** Same grid, same resolution, same vertical structure, same +120 h range at all four cycles. The published **parameter set is thinner**: 102 folders against the deterministic model's 132.
  - **35 parameters are deterministic-only**, of which one is an ART field — **`DUST_MAX_TOTAL_MC_LAYER`** (layer-maximum total dust mass concentration) is absent from the ensemble, the same omission as on the global feed. The other 34 are meteorological: aviation and visibility diagnostics (`CEILING`, `VIS`, `CLDEPTH`, `HTOP_DC`, `HZEROCL`, `CAPE_MU`, `DEN`, `CLCT_MOD`), lake-model fields (`FR_LAKE`, `DEPTH_LK`, `H_ML_LK`, `C_T_LK`, `T_BOT_LK`, `T_MNW_LK`, `T_WML_LK`), surface and vegetation fields (`LAI`, `PLCOV`, `ROOTDP`, `RSTOM`, `SOILTYP`, `SMI`, `EVAP_PL`), snow and ice detail (`RHO_SNOW`, `SNOAG`, `HSNOW_MAX`, `ALB_SEAICE`, `W_SO_ICE`), turbulent exchange coefficients (`TCH`, `TCM`), fluxes (`ALHFL_BS`, `ALHFL_S`, `ASHFL_S`), and column hydrometeors (`TQR`, `TQS`).
  - **5 parameters are ensemble-only:** `FI`, `RELHUM`, `OMEGA` (all pressure-level), `SOBS_RAD`, and `THBS_RAD`.
  - **Cycle coverage is nearly complete**, unlike the global ART ensemble. 93 of 102 parameters appear in all four cycles. Nine appear at **00 and 12 UTC only**: `FI`, `RELHUM`, `OMEGA`, `TQV`, `TQC`, `TQI`, `RAIN_CON`, `RAIN_GSP`, `HBAS_CON` — the pressure-level fields, the column water species, and the convective/grid-scale precipitation split. Four more are present at 06/18 on a thinner schedule (see *06 / 18 UTC cycles*). **Every dust parameter is present in every cycle, on the same steps.**

- **Member packaging:** **One file per member**, as a directory segment `…/r/{run}/e/{NN}/s/{step}.grib2` — not a member dimension inside a combined file, and not the packed multi-member files used on the older `/weather/nwp/` tier. DWD lists robust handling of late or failed members as an explicit design goal of this URL scheme; the consequence is that **a missing member is simply an absent directory**. Enumerate `e/` rather than assuming ten. Ensemble encoding: `productDefinitionTemplateNumber` 1 / 41 / 49 / 58 / 68 depending on parameter class, `typeOfProcessedData = cp`, and `typeOfEnsembleForecast = 192` — a value in the 192–254 range reserved for local use, so it carries no portable meaning and cannot discriminate control from perturbed. (The deterministic sibling uses PDT 48 and `typeOfProcessedData = fc`.)

- **Derived products distributed:** **None.** No ensemble mean, spread, quantile, or exceedance-probability fields — only raw members. Any probabilistic dust product must be computed after fetching all ten.

## What it provides

**27 ART mineral-dust parameters** plus a standard meteorological set, all on the native triangular grid.

### ART dust parameters

| Group | Parameters | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Optical depth | `TAOD_DUST` | Column-integrated, dimensionless (`Numeric`) |
| Optical depth (layered) | `AER_DUST` | GRIB `shortName` is `AOD_DUST`; per model layer |
| Mass concentration | `DUST_TOTAL_MC`, `DUST_TOTAL_MC_VI` | kg m⁻³ (3D) and kg m⁻² (column) |
| Prognostic modes | `DUSTA`, `DUSTB`, `DUSTC` | kg kg⁻¹ mass mixing ratio, fine / medium / coarse |
| Mode number/aux | `DUSTA0`, `DUSTB0`, `DUSTC0` | |
| Accumulated emission | `ACCEMISS_DUST{A,B,C}` | kg m⁻², `stepType = accum` |
| Accumulated dry deposition | `ACCDRYDEPO_DUST{A,B,C}` | kg m⁻², accumulated |
| Accumulated wet deposition | `ACCWETDEPO_CON_DUST{A,B,C}`, `ACCWETDEPO_GSP_DUST{A,B,C}` | Convective and grid-scale, separately |

| Emission diagnostics | `USTAR`, `USTAR_THRES` | m s⁻¹ — friction velocity and its emission threshold |

Meteorological output covers the usual near-surface and screen-level set (2 m temperature and dewpoint, 10 m wind and gusts, MSLP, surface pressure, cloud cover by layer, total and phase-split precipitation, radiation budget terms, soil temperature and moisture, snow and sea-ice fields, CAPE, column water species) plus, in the 00/12 cycles, a short pressure-level set.

Only a fraction of the 74 model levels is published, and the subset differs sharply between dust and meteorology:

| Field group | Levels published |

| `W_SO` | 8 soil depths: 0.0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.09, 0.27, 0.81, 2.43, 7.29 m |

> **The prognostic dust modes are surface-only in practice.** `DUSTA`/`B`/`C` — the actual prognostic variables — are published on three near-surface levels, while the *diagnostic* `DUST_TOTAL_MC` gets 44. Vertical dust structure is available as total mass concentration and backscatter, not resolved by mode. Identical to the global ensemble.

> **The pressure-level set is shallower than the global ensemble's.** Nine levels from 300 hPa down, against nineteen levels from 1 hPa down on [ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md). There is no stratospheric or upper-tropospheric pressure-level output here, and these fields are absent entirely from the 06/18 cycles.

## Data availability
- **Is the data free?** Yes
- **License:** **CC BY 4.0**, attribution required. DWD's legal notice places all its open spatial data and its EU High Value Datasets under CC BY 4.0 with source acknowledgement.
- **Is the data downloadable?** Yes — plain HTTP, no registration
- **Data format:** GRIB2, **uncompressed** (no `.bz2` wrapper on this tier). Internal `grid_ccsds` (CCSDS/AEC lossless) packing at `bitsPerValue = 16`, no bitmap. `tablesVersion = 19`, `localTablesVersion = 1`, `centre = edzw`.
- **Official download location:**
  https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/nwp/v1/m/icon-art-eu-eps/p/
- **Path structure:** three variants depending on the parameter's dimensionality —
  - `…/p/{PARAM}/r/{run}/e/{NN}/s/PT{hhh}H{mm}M.grib2` — single-level fields
  - `…/p/{PARAM}/lvt1/{levelType}/lv1/{level}/r/{run}/e/{NN}/s/PT{hhh}H{mm}M.grib2` — multi-level fields
  - `…/p/{PARAM}/wvl1/{nm}/lvt1/{levelType}/lv1/{level}/r/{run}/e/{NN}/s/PT{hhh}H{mm}M.grib2` — `CEIL_BSC_DUST` and `SAT_BSC_DUST` only

  where `{levelType}` is the numeric GRIB2 `typeOfFirstFixedSurface` (100 = isobaric, 106 = depth below land surface, 150 = generalized vertical height coordinate), `{run}` is ISO-8601 (`2026-10-18T12:00`, URL-encoded as `2026-10-18T12%3A00`), and `{NN}` is the zero-padded member number.

  Example: `https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/nwp/v1/m/icon-art-eu-eps/p/TAOD_DUST/r/2026-10-18T12%3A00/e/01/s/PT024H00M.grib2`

  **Requesting `…/{PARAM}/r/` on a multi-level parameter returns 404, not an empty listing** — the `lvt1/` segment is mandatory there. Probe `…/p/{PARAM}/` first to determine which variant applies.

- **Decoding:** DWD specifies its GRIB definitions for ecCodes v2.32.0 or newer. Verification for this entry decoded all sampled messages, including the ART parameter names, with stock ecCodes 2.48.0.
- **Run volume (server manifest, 2026-10-18/19, all 10 members):** ~134 GB / 139,500 files per 00/12 UTC run; ~101.5 GB / 107,520 files per 06/18 UTC run.
- **Retention (observed):** Roughly 24 hours of rolling coverage — four cycles present at any time. Older runs retain their **complete** step list; **no pruning of elapsed lead times** (re-confirmed 2026-10-19). The deterministic [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md) and [ICON-ART](../../global/germany/icon-art.md) feeds have since been checked and behave the same way; their entries no longer describe pruning.

## Notes

- **The domain and resolution are the most commonly mis-stated facts about this system.** It is *not* the ~6.5 km ICON-EU nest. It is a dedicated aerosol domain spanning the equator to 82°N and 70.6°W to 70.6°E — DWD's ICON-ART-EU-NA² — at ~10 km on grid #61 since 6 October 2026 (~13 km on grid #51, built in February 2022, before). The deterministic [ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md) was verified to use the same grid #61 and the same 1,013,404 cells.

- **The 6 October 2026 grid change splits any archive.** Grid #51 (13 km) before, grid #61 (10 km) after. Check `numberOfGridUsed` before stacking data across that date or building a climatology of member spread.

- **Dust is available in all four cycles here, unlike the global ensemble.** [ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md) publishes ART parameters at 00 and 12 UTC only, with meteorology-only 06/18 cycles. This feed carries dust four times a day, which makes it the better choice for anyone needing dust guidance refreshed more often than twice daily — at the cost of a 120 h ceiling against the global ensemble's 180 h at 00/12.

- **The 06/18 cycles are near-complete but not identical.** 93 of 102 parameters; missing are the three pressure-level fields, the three column water species, the convective/grid-scale rain split, and `HBAS_CON`, and `CAPE_ML`, `SNOW_CON`/`SNOW_GSP` and `W_SO` are on thinner schedules. Pipelines depending on pressure-level output, `RAIN_CON`/`RAIN_GSP` or soil moisture must poll 00/12.

- **Documentation-vs-reality discrepancies in DWD's announcement.** The April 2025 Open Data announcement is the only substantive public description of these systems, and one of its statements does not match the feed: it states temporal resolution is "as a rule hourly to +51 h, then 3-hourly." The dominant meteorological schedule here is hourly to **+75 h** then 3-hourly, `TAOD_DUST` breaks at +72 h, and eleven further schedules exist that the sentence does not describe. The announcement is accurate on member count (10), the NA² domain, +120 h range, native-grid-only distribution, per-member file splitting, and the absence of bzip2. Its resolution (~13 km, Grid #51) was correct at the time but has been superseded by the 6 October 2026 upgrade to ~10 km, grid #61.

- **The ensemble is not coarser than the deterministic run.** Same grid #61, same 1,013,404 cells, same 74 levels, same +120 h range at all four cycles. As with the global ART pair, this makes the deterministic/ensemble comparison unusually clean for spread–error analysis — no resolution confound.

- **`numberOfForecastsInEnsemble` is trustworthy here.** It reports 10 and ten members are published. Worth stating because the equivalent key is unreliable in ECMWF's post-Cycle-50r1 open data (see [ENS-WAM](../../../wave_models/global/eu/ecwam-ens.md)); no such inconsistency exists on this feed.

- **`typeOfEnsembleForecast = 192` is a local code.** It sits in the WMO range reserved for local use and has no published DWD interpretation, so it conveys nothing portable. Size member arrays from the `perturbationNumber` values actually present, or from the `e/` directory listing.

- **Folder name ≠ GRIB shortName for `AER_DUST`.** The directory is `AER_DUST`; messages decode as `AOD_DUST`. Keyed lookups built from directory names will miss.

- **Public data is a subset of the operational system.** 102 of the deterministic model's 132 parameter folders, most 3D fields cut to three near-surface levels, a nine-level pressure set, and no derived ensemble products.

## Relationship to other models

- **[ICON-ART-EU](./icon-art-eu.md):** The deterministic regional sibling — same grid #61, same 1,013,404 cells, same resolution and vertical structure, same +120 h range and four cycles. Differences are in publication only: 132 parameters against 102, and `DUST_MAX_TOTAL_MC_LAYER`, `CEILING`, `VIS`, `CAPE_MU`, and `DEN` present only in the deterministic feed. `VIS` is removed from the deterministic feed after 30 November 2026 (131 folders from then on).
- **[ICON-ART-EPS](../../global/germany/icon-art-eps.md):** The global ensemble counterpart, also 10 members, on grid #39 (~20 km) since 6 October 2026 — grid #36, the declared parent of grid #51, before. Runs to +180 h at 00/12 UTC but publishes dust only in those two cycles.
- **[ICON-ART](../../global/germany/icon-art.md):** The global deterministic dust forecast.
- **[ICON-ART Pollen](./icon-art-pollen.md):** DWD's limited-area ICON-ART pollen forecast — a separate configuration on a separate channel (daily-mean NetCDF under `/climate_environment/health/forecasts/pollen/`). It has **no ensemble counterpart**; the `v1/m/` tier carries only the four dust configurations.
- **[ICON-EU-EPS](../../../ensemble_models/regional/de/icon-eu-eps.md):** DWD's 40-member European meteorological ensemble, on grid #63 (342,428 cells, ~10 km) since 6 October 2026 (grid #37, 164,984 cells, ~13 km before). Despite matching nominal resolutions both before and after the upgrade, this is a **different grid over a different domain** — the ICON-EU nest against the larger aerosol domain. The two are not interchangeable and their cells do not correspond.
- **[CAMS Regional](../eu/cams-regional.md):** The European multi-model air quality ensemble, whose spread is structural rather than perturbation-based and whose species coverage is far wider. For probabilistic European air quality beyond dust, CAMS Regional rather than ICON-ART-EU-EPS is the relevant system; CAMS Regional's dust fields make the two directly comparable on that one species.

## Recent version history

ICON-ART-EU-EPS has no independent change-notice series. It is upgraded as part of the wider ICON system; see the [ICON Global](../../../nwp_models/global/germany/icon-global.md#recent-version-history) and [ICON-EPS](../../../ensemble_models/global/de/icon-eps.md#recent-version-history) entries for the full sequence.

### After 30 November 2026 — `/weather/lib/cdo/` removed
Announced in DWD's Open Data newsletter of 2 September 2026. This feed is already on the v1 URL scheme and never carried `VIS`, so the shutdown of the legacy ICON trees and the withdrawal of visibility on that date do not affect its contents. (`VIS` is removed from the deterministic ICON-ART-EU feed at that date.)

### 6 October 2026 — resolution increase (effective 06 UTC run)
Per DWD's change notice of 6 October 2026 and the 28 July 2026 newsletter: ICON-ART refined from 13 km to 10 km in the ICON-ART-EU-NA² nest (grid #51 → grid #61, 1.78× the grid points) and from 26 km to 20 km globally (grid #36 → grid #39), for the deterministic forecast and the 10 ensemble members alike. Vertical resolution unchanged; the NA² domain unchanged. `CLAT`/`CLON`/`ELAT`/`ELON` changed with the grid. Test data for the 2026-07-15 00 UTC run were offered beforehand under `https://opendata.dwd.de/test/weather/nwp/v1/m/icon-art-eu-eps/`. The CRPS gains DWD reports in the notice (typically 1–3% at the surface and in the lower troposphere) are for ICON-EPS meteorology; no dust-specific scores were given.

### 16 June 2026 — CCSDS packing
DWD switched ICON-family GRIB2 output to CCSDS packing. On this tier files are distributed uncompressed, so the change is visible directly as the `grid_ccsds` packing type rather than through a `.bz2` wrapper.

### April 2025 — publication on DWD Open Data
DWD published the ICON-ART NWP system — global ICON-ART and ICON-ART-EPS, regional ICON-ART-EU and ICON-ART-EU-EPS — under `/weather/nwp/v1/m/`, introducing both the ART mineral-dust parameter set and DWD's revised URL scheme (ISO-8601 run and step encoding, per-member file splitting, native-grid-only distribution, GRIB-internal compression in place of bzip2).

### 28 February 2022 — grid #51 generated
The R3B07 Europe / North Atlantic / North Africa mesh used by this system was created as the 13 km successor to grid #50 (R2B07, 20 km, October 2021), explicitly as the larger nest for prognostic aerosol forecasting with ICON-ART.

### November 2023 — ICON-ART operational at DWD
Mineral dust forecasting with ICON-ART became technically operational, after several years of quasi-operational use under the PerduS (Prediction of Saharan Dust) project.
