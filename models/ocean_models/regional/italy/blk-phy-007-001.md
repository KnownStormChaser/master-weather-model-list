# BLK-PHY NRT (Black Sea Physics Analysis and Forecast)

## What this model is
BLK-PHY NRT is the physical component of the **Black Sea Forecasting System**, the operational ocean physics product of the Copernicus Marine **Black Sea Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (BS MFC)**, produced at **CMCC** in Italy.

Structurally it is not one model but **two**, distributed under a single product identifier:

- **Black Sea** — a **NEMO v4.2.2** configuration at 1/40° (~2.5 km) with **121 native z-levels**, coupled to WAVEWATCH III, with **OceanVar** 3D-Var assimilation. Includes the Bosphorus Strait and a portion of the Marmara Sea for the Mediterranean interface, and delivers a **sea-ice** dataset.
- **Marmara Sea** — the **Unstructured Turkish Straits System (U-TSS)** model, built on **SHYFEM** with an unstructured mesh from 50 m near the coast to 500 m offshore, 93 levels, interpolated onto a regular 1/160° (~500 m) grid for delivery. No data assimilation.

The two sub-systems have different resolutions, different vertical grids, different model cores, different delivery times, different forecast lengths, and different archive extents. **They share only the product identifier.**

The official Copernicus Marine product identifier is `BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_007_001`. The PUM calls the Black Sea component **BLK-PHY NRT**; the Marmara component is **U-TSS**.

> **As in the Mediterranean, the wave model inside this product is not the distributed wave product.** The PUM gives the model version as `NEMO v4.2.2 - WW3 v6.07.1`, so the physics carries an internal WAVEWATCH III. The Copernicus Black Sea wave product, [BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md), is a **WAM Cycle 6** system run by Hereon in Germany. See *Coupling*.

*Verified 2026-08-25 from the PUM (Issue 5.0, November 2025), Marine Data Store STAC records, and live ARCO Zarr metadata.*

## Who runs it
- **Production Unit:** **CMCC** — Centro Euro-Mediterraneo sui Cambiamenti Climatici, Italy, for both sub-systems
- **Country:** Italy; domain covers the Black Sea, Sea of Azov, Turkish Straits, and Marmara Sea
- **Programme or coordinating body:** Copernicus Marine Service — Black Sea Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (BS MFC)
- **Role in any larger system:** supplies sea surface height and surface currents to [BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md); supplies the Dardanelles boundary to [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md); the U-TSS component supplies this product's own Bosphorus boundary

CMCC runs both this and [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) — the only Production Unit operating two Copernicus Marine regional physics products, and the reason the Aegean–Black Sea exchange is handled internally rather than across institutes.

## What area it covers

### Black Sea sub-system
- **Coverage:** the full Black Sea basin, the Sea of Azov, the Bosphorus Strait, and a portion of the Marmara Sea
- **Domain bounds (documented and live-verified, in agreement):** **27.25°E – 42.00°E, 40.50°N – 47.325°N**
- **Grid dimensions (live-verified):** **591 × 274** (longitude × latitude) — matching the PUM
- **Grid spacing (live-verified):** 0.025° in both directions, i.e. 1/40°
- **Identical grid to [BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md)**, the Black Sea wave product — same bounds, same 591 × 274 dimensions. The physics and wave products *are* directly combinable here, unlike the Mediterranean pair.

### Marmara Sea sub-system
- **Coverage:** the Marmara basin and the Turkish Straits
- **Domain bounds (documented):** 25°E – 30°E, 39.5°N – 41.5°N
- **Grid dimensions (live-verified):** **801 × 321** (longitude × latitude). *The PUM says "320 x 800" — off by one in each direction, the usual fencepost error.*
- **Grid spacing (live-verified):** 0.00625°, i.e. 1/160° (~500 m)
- **Native grid:** unstructured SHYFEM mesh, **50 m near the coast to 500 m in deeper areas**, interpolated to the regular grid above for delivery. The native mesh resolution is not recoverable from the delivered product.

The product-level STAC bbox is **[25, 39.5, 42, 47.325]** — the union of the two sub-domains, which describes neither one accurately.

## Forcing

### Black Sea
- **Atmospheric forcing:** **ECMWF at 1/10°** — 6-hourly analysis; **hourly for forecast days 1–3, 3-hourly for days 4–6, 6-hourly for days 7–10**. The same schedule as [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) and [BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md).
- **River runoff:** **90 rivers forced at daily frequency.** The **Danube** uses observations from **NIHWM** (Romania's National Institute of Hydrology and Water Management) *including forecast data*; all remaining rivers use **EFAS v5**. The Danube's separate treatment reflects its dominance of the Black Sea freshwater budget.
- **Lateral boundary conditions:** **three open boundaries at the Marmara Sea**, forced with data from the **U-TSS model** — i.e. from this product's own Marmara sub-system
- **Tidal forcing:** explicit, **8 constituents — M2, S2, N2, K2, K1, O1, P1, Q1** — identical to the Mediterranean set, and again a small set for a nearly non-tidal basin that nonetheless ships de-tided datasets

### Marmara Sea (U-TSS)
- **Atmospheric forcing:** ECMWF at 1/10°, **3-hourly analysis and forecast**
- **Lateral boundary conditions:** **Black Sea and Mediterranean MFC products** — see the circular dependency described in *Relationship to other entries*

## Coupling

The PUM describes the Black Sea sub-system as a **hydrodynamic-wave model** with version `NEMO v4.2.2 - WW3 v6.07.1`.

> **Two wave models operate in the Black Sea MFC, exactly as in the Mediterranean.**
>
> | | **WW3 inside BLK-PHY** | **[BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md)** |
> |---|---|---|
> | Model core | WAVEWATCH III v6.07.1 | WAM Cycle 6 (shallow-water) |
> | Operator | CMCC (Italy) | Hereon (Germany) |
> | Product | Internal to `..._PHY_007_001` | `BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_007_003` |
> | Distributed as waves? | **No** | Yes |
> | Data assimilation | — | Sequential OI of altimetric SWH and wind speed |
>
> **This resolves an open point in the BSeas7 entry.** That entry notes *"Coupling is listed as N/A in the PUM — unlike IBIWAM or AMM15-WW3, there is no two-way feedback from the wave model back into the Black Sea physics system."* That is correct **about BSeas7** — Hereon's WAM does not feed back. But it is easy to read as "the Black Sea physics has no wave coupling," which is not the case: the physics carries its own internal WW3. The BSeas7 entry is worth annotating so the two statements do not appear to conflict.

**As with the Mediterranean, the coupling details are undocumented.** The PUM names WW3 v6.07.1 and calls the system a hydrodynamic-wave model, but specifies no coupler, no coupling frequency, no exchanged fields, and no direction. The identical gap in two CMCC-run products suggests a shared documentation practice rather than an oversight in one PUM (*TBD*).

The Marmara sub-system is standalone SHYFEM with no wave component. There is no atmospheric coupling in either sub-system.

## Data assimilation

**Black Sea only.** The Marmara sub-system runs without assimilation.

- **DA scheme:** **OceanVar**, a **3D-Var** variational scheme developed by CMCC. *Note this is OceanVar, not the **OceanVar2** used by [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) — the two CMCC products are on different versions of the same scheme.*
- **Daily cycle:** 3 days of analysis, 1 day of simulation, 10 days of forecast
- **Weekly cycle:** each **Tuesday**, 14 days of analysis, 1 day of simulation, 10 days of forecast, "to assimilate a larger number of in-situ and satellite observations"

### Assimilated observations
- **In-situ vertical profiles** of temperature and salinity from **Argo**
- **Sea level anomaly** from available satellites (the PUM does not enumerate missions, unlike the Mediterranean PUM)
- **Sea surface temperature** — gridded, from Copernicus TACs

The observation list is notably thinner than the Mediterranean product's, and no Copernicus product identifiers are given for any of the three streams (**TBD**).

> ### ⚠ De-tided variables carry the same names as the tidal ones
> Live-verified 2026-08-25: `cmems_mod_blk_phy-cur_anfc_detided-2.5km_P1D-m` contains **`uo` and `vo` with standard names `eastward_sea_water_velocity` and `northward_sea_water_velocity`**, and `cmems_mod_blk_phy-ssh_anfc_detided-2.5km_P1D-m` contains **`zos` with `sea_surface_height_above_geoid`**.
>
> There is **no `_detided` suffix and no `_assuming_no_tide` standard name anywhere in the file.** A de-tided Black Sea file is indistinguishable from a tidal one by variable name, standard name, or units — **only the dataset identifier tells you which you have.**
>
> This is a third convention within the same programme. [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) and [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md) both use `uo_detided` / `zos_detided` with `_assuming_no_tide` standard names; the [Baltic](../sweden/bal-mfc-nemo.md) uses `uos_detided` / `zos_detided`. Anyone building a cross-basin de-tided pipeline must handle all three, and the Black Sea case is the one that fails silently.

**`wo` ships inside the `cur` datasets** on the same 121-level tracer grid — not as a separate `wcur` dataset the way [Med](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) and [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md) do it, and without IBI's W-point offset. **`bottomT` ships inside the `temp` datasets.**

**Sea ice is a Black Sea reality.** The `ice` dataset carries `siconc` and `sithick` at daily frequency — the northwestern shelf and Sea of Azov freeze in cold winters. Note `siconc` has an **empty units attribute**.

## Relationship to other entries

- **Wave counterpart — with the Mediterranean caveat:** **[BSeas7](../../../wave_models/regional/germany/bseas7-blk.md)** (`BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_007_003`) takes sea surface height and surface currents from this product, one-way. It is a WAM Cycle 6 system run by Hereon, **not** the WW3 that this product's physics is coupled to. Unlike the Mediterranean pair, the two Black Sea products are on an **identical grid** (591 × 274, same bounds), so they combine without regridding.

- **A three-way circular dependency across the Turkish Straits.** This is the most tangled boundary arrangement in the Copernicus regional portfolio:
  - The **Black Sea** sub-system takes its Marmara boundaries from the **U-TSS** model
  - **U-TSS** takes its boundaries from the **Black Sea and Mediterranean** MFC products
  - **[Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md)** takes its Dardanelles boundary from **this product** plus a monthly Marmara climatology

  So Black Sea ← U-TSS ← {Black Sea, Mediterranean}, and Mediterranean ← Black Sea. Both loops run inside CMCC, which is presumably how they are sequenced without deadlock, but **no PUM documents the ordering** (*TBD*). Note also that the Marmara climatology the Mediterranean uses (Maderich et al. 2015) is a **different model** from the U-TSS output shipped inside this product — the two Marmara representations in the Copernicus catalogue are not the same thing.

- **Same operator as the Mediterranean.** [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) is the other CMCC regional physics product. The two share the 8-constituent tidal set, the ECMWF forcing schedule, EFAS v5 river input, and the undocumented-internal-WW3 pattern — but differ on assimilation version (OceanVar here, OceanVar2 there), CF convention version, and de-tided naming.

- **Indirect dependency on EFAS.** 89 of 90 rivers use **EFAS v5**, with the Danube handled separately from NIHWM observations and forecasts. As with the Mediterranean, this routes an excluded product's output into an included one — worth the same note in [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md).

- **Which to use when:** for the Black Sea and Azov this is the only Copernicus physics option. For the Turkish Straits and Marmara specifically, the `mrm-500m` datasets inside this product are the highest-resolution operational representation available openly — but they carry no assimilation and a shorter forecast.

- **Programme context:** see [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md) for the full Copernicus Marine portfolio.

## Notes

- **This is two models in one product.** The Black Sea NEMO configuration and the Marmara U-TSS/SHYFEM configuration share nothing but a product identifier — different cores, grids, vertical levels, forecast lengths, delivery times, assimilation status, and archive extents. Anyone treating `BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_007_001` as a single homogeneous product will get this wrong.

- **De-tided variables are unlabelled.** See the warning under *What it provides*. The most consequential thing to know about this product, and the case most likely to cause silent errors in a cross-basin workflow.

- **The internal WW3 is not BSeas7**, and the BSeas7 entry's "coupling N/A" note is about BSeas7 rather than about the physics. See *Coupling*.

- **The Marmara grid is 801 × 321, not 320 × 800.** The PUM's figure is off by one in each direction. Its printed 93-level list also stops at 2100 m where the delivered grid reaches **2200 m** — one level short.

- **`siconc` has an empty units attribute.** It should be dimensionless (`1`), as it is in the [TOPAZ5](../norway/topaz5.md) and [Baltic](../sweden/bal-mfc-nemo.md) products. Minor, but it will trip strict CF validators.

- **The Marmara sub-system has a longer archive than the Black Sea one.** 2021-11 against 2023-11 for daily fields. Counter-intuitive for the secondary component, and a consequence of the Black Sea datasets being re-delivered at the May 2025 production change.

- **Two different Marmara Sea representations exist in the Copernicus catalogue.** The U-TSS output shipped here, and the Maderich et al. (2015) monthly climatology used by [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md) at its Dardanelles boundary. They are not the same model and are not mutually consistent.

- **The Danube gets its own forcing chain.** NIHWM observations plus forecast, separate from the EFAS v5 treatment of the other 89 rivers. Given the Danube's share of Black Sea freshwater input this is a meaningful design choice and makes the product more responsive to real Danube flood events than a pure EFAS chain would be.

- **The assimilated observation list is thin and unidentified.** "Sea Level Anomaly (SLA) from available satellites" with no missions named, and no Copernicus product identifiers for any of the three observation streams — against the Mediterranean PUM, which names nine altimeters and four product IDs. Whether this reflects a genuinely smaller observing system or just less documentation is unclear (**TBD**).

- **CF-1.6 is the oldest convention version in the regional physics portfolio.** Med and IBI are at CF-1.8, NWS at CF-1.7. Worth checking whether the delivered files actually declare 1.6 or whether the PUM is stale.

- **Every day rewrites the previous three days, and Tuesdays rewrite fourteen.** The same incremental-rewrite behaviour documented for [Med-Physics](../italy/med-phy-006-013.md), with a longer Tuesday look-back. Naive append-only archiving is unsafe.

- **De-tided datasets are one day shorter**, ending 2026-09-01 against 2026-09-02, and the PUM states the de-tided forecast is 9 days against 10 — the expected Doodson-filter cost, consistent across the Baltic, IBI, and Mediterranean products.
