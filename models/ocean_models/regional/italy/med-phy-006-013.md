# Med-Physics (Mediterranean Sea Physics Analysis and Forecast)

## What this model is
Med-Physics is the physical component of the **Mediterranean Forecasting System (MFS)**, the operational ocean physics product of the Copernicus Marine **Mediterranean Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (Med MFC)**, produced at **CMCC** in Italy.

It is a **NEMO v4.2** configuration at 1/24° (~4 km) with **141 vertical levels** — by a wide margin the finest vertical resolution in the Copernicus Marine regional physics portfolio — delivered on the model's native z\* grid. Tides are explicit (8 constituents), and the solution is corrected by **OceanVar2**, a 3D-Var scheme assimilating T/S profiles and along-track altimetry.

The system is described as a **coupled hydrodynamic-wave modelling system**: the PUM gives the model version as **`NEMO v4.2 - WW3 v6.07`**, i.e. NEMO coupled to WAVEWATCH III.

> **This means the Mediterranean MFC operates two different wave models.** The WW3 v6.07 component inside Med-Physics is not [MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md), which is a **WAM Cycle 6** system run by HCMR in Greece and distributed as `MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_006_017`. The two also cover different domains. See *Coupling* — this is the single most important structural fact about this product and it is easy to get wrong.

The official Copernicus Marine product identifier is `MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_006_013`. The system is called **Med-Physics** or **Med-PHY**; there is no shorter version-tagged name in current use.

*Verified 2026-08-25 from the PUM (Issue 2.5, November 2025), Marine Data Store STAC records, and live ARCO Zarr metadata.*

---

## Who runs it
- **Production Unit:** **CMCC** — Centro Euro-Mediterraneo sui Cambiamenti Climatici, Italy
- **Country:** Italy; domain covers the whole Mediterranean basin and an Atlantic box
- **Programme or coordinating body:** Copernicus Marine Service — Mediterranean Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (Med MFC)
- **Role in any larger system:** supplies hourly surface currents and sea level to [MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md); the Med MFC's physical backbone, alongside the Mediterranean biogeochemistry product `MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_BGC_006_014`

CMCC also runs the [Black Sea physics product](./blk-phy-007-001.md) (`BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_007_001`) — the only Production Unit operating two Copernicus Marine regional physics products.

---

## What area it covers
- **Coverage:** the entire Mediterranean Sea, plus an adjacent box in the Atlantic west of Gibraltar
- **Domain bounds (documented and live-verified, in agreement):** **17.2917°W – 36.2917°E, 30.1875°N – 45.9792°N**
- **Grid dimensions (live-verified):** **1287 × 380** (longitude × latitude) — matching the PUM's "1287 x 380 x 141"
- **Grid spacing (live-verified):** 0.0416667° in both directions, i.e. 1/24°
- **Native grid:** staggered Arakawa C-grid with land masking. All variables are delivered **interpolated to the centre of the tracer grid cell**.
- **Eastern boundary:** the Dardanelles Strait is treated as a forced boundary, not a closed wall — see *Forcing*

> **The physics and wave domains differ.** Med-Physics runs from **17.2917°W**; [MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md) runs from **18.125°W**, roughly 0.83° further into the Atlantic, giving it 1307 longitude points against this product's 1287. The wave model needs the extra western fetch to capture Atlantic swell propagating toward Gibraltar. The two products are therefore **not on a common grid** despite sharing a resolution and a coordinating centre — anything combining them needs regridding.

## Basic details
- **Model type:** Regional ocean physics; deterministic; coupled hydrodynamic-wave; with data assimilation
- **Core ocean model:** **NEMO v4.2**
- **Wave component:** **WAVEWATCH III v6.07** (internal to the coupled system — see *Coupling*)
- **Sea ice model:** None (domain is ice-free)
- **System name:** Med-Physics (Med-PHY)
- **Horizontal resolution:** 1/24° (~4 km)
- **Vertical levels:** **141**, delivered on the **model's native grid**, 1.018 m – 5754.044 m (live-verified). First levels: 1.018, 3.166, 5.465, 7.920, 10.537, 13.318, 16.271, 19.398 m; deepest: 5433.461, 5539.333, 5646.199, **5754.044** m.
- **Vertical coordinate:** **z\*** (time-varying thickness) internally, delivered along a fixed z-level grid. **Partial cells** are used, so the last ocean level thickness varies to follow the model topography.
- **Forecast length:** **10 days**, initialised daily
- **Update frequency:** daily forecast cycle; weekly analysis cycle
- **Production cycles:** daily cycle starts at **08:00 UTC**; weekly analysis cycle starts **Mondays at 12:00 UTC**
- **Target delivery time:** **16:00 UTC daily** for forecast, daily and hourly datasets; **Tuesdays at 16:00 UTC** for the weekly analysis. Monthly datasets update on the **20th** of each month with the previous month's mean.
- **Temporal output resolution:** 15-minute instantaneous (surface), hourly mean (2D and 3D), daily mean, monthly mean, plus separate de-tided daily datasets
- **Archive availability:** documented as a 2-year rolling window; live extents vary substantially by dataset — see *Data availability*
- **Bathymetry source:** **GEBCO 30 arc-second**, interpolated onto the model grid
- **Initial conditions (spin-up):** World Ocean Atlas (WOA) 2013 V2 winter climatology, from 1 January 2015

## Forcing
- **Atmospheric forcing:** **ECMWF at 1/10°** — 6-hourly analysis; **hourly for forecast days 1–3, 3-hourly for days 4–6, and 6-hourly for days 7–10**. The degradation schedule matches the one used by [MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md), as both draw on the same ECMWF stream.
- **River runoff:** **EFAS v5** — the Copernicus Emergency Management Service European Flood Awareness System. A daily-updated hydrological forecast rather than a climatology, which distinguishes this product from [AMM15](../uk/amm15-nws.md) and [AMM7](../uk/amm7-nws-lr.md), both of which use frozen river climatologies. *Note that EFAS's own medium-range forecast is excluded from this repository for embargo reasons — see [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md) — but its output reaches users indirectly through this product.*
- **Lateral boundary conditions:**
  - **Atlantic:** **[GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md)** (`GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024`)
  - **Dardanelles Strait:** a combination of **monthly climatological fields from a Marmara Sea model** (Maderich et al. 2015) and **daily fields from the Black Sea analysis and forecast product** (`BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_007_001`)
- **Tidal forcing:** explicit, **8 constituents — M2, S2, N2, K2, K1, O1, P1, Q1**. The smallest tidal set among the Copernicus regional physics products (against 11 for IBI and AMM15, 16 for AMM7), reflecting the Mediterranean's weak tides — though the product still ships de-tided datasets.
- **Surface heat flux correction:** Objective Analysis SST fields relax surface heat fluxes with a constant of **110 W m⁻² K⁻¹**, applied close to midnight

## Coupling

The PUM describes Med-Physics as a **coupled hydrodynamic-wave modelling system** and gives the model version as **`NEMO v4.2 - WW3 v6.07`**.

> **Two distinct wave models operate in the Mediterranean MFC, and conflating them is the main hazard here.**
>
> | | **WW3 inside Med-Physics** | **[MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md)** |
> |---|---|---|
> | Model core | WAVEWATCH III v6.07 | WAM Cycle 6 (MEDWAM4) |
> | Operator | CMCC (Italy) | HCMR (Greece) |
> | Product | Internal to `..._PHY_006_013` | `MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_006_017` |
> | Western bound | 17.2917°W | 18.125°W |
> | Distributed as waves? | **No** | Yes |
>
> MEDWAM ingests hourly surface currents and sea level from this product, so the two systems are linked — but MEDWAM is **not** the wave half of this coupled system. The WW3 component's wave fields are not distributed anywhere; only the physics it influences is.

**The coupling details are not documented.** The PUM names WW3 v6.07 and calls the system coupled, but says nothing about the coupler (OASIS or otherwise), the coupling frequency, the exchanged fields, or whether the exchange is one-way or two-way. The glossary defines WW3 and the version appears in the production table; that is the entire treatment. Compare the [IBI physics PUM](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md), which enumerates its three wave-forcing processes and names the source fields, and the [AMM15 PUM](../uk/amm15-nws.md), which specifies OASIS3-MCT at hourly frequency with three named effects. **This is a documentation gap worth raising with the Med MFC** (*TBD*).

There is no atmospheric coupling — ECMWF forcing is one-way. The Mediterranean biogeochemistry product (`MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_BGC_006_014`) is a separate production, not online-coupled the way the Baltic and NWS-LR systems are.

## Data assimilation
- **DA scheme:** **OceanVar2**, a **3D-Var** variational scheme
- **Assimilation cycle:** **daily (24 h), in filter mode** — note this contrasts with the "Update frequency: Weekly" line in the General Information table, which describes the *analysis product* delivery cadence, not the assimilation frequency
- **Daily cycle:** each day J the product is updated with an adjusted analysis for the previous three days (J−3 to J−1) plus a 10-day forecast
- **Weekly cycle:** each Tuesday the product is updated with a best adjusted analysis covering days J−15 to J−9, followed by an adjusted analysis covering J−8 to J−1 and a 10-day forecast
- **Surface heat flux correction:** separate from the variational analysis — Objective Analysis SST relaxation at 110 W m⁻² K⁻¹ near midnight

### Assimilated observations
- **Sea level anomaly:** along-track SLA from Altika, CryoSat, Jason-2, Jason-3, HY-2A, HY-2B, Sentinel-3A, Sentinel-3B, and Sentinel-6A, via `SEALEVEL_EUR_PHY_L3_MY_008_061` and `SEALEVEL_EUR_PHY_L3_NRT_008_059`
- **Sea surface temperature:** L4 fields from `SST_MED_SST_L4_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_010_004` — used to correct surface heat fluxes rather than assimilated into the state vector
- **In-situ profiles:** temperature and salinity from **Argo floats and gliders**, temperature from **XBTs**, via `INSITU_MED_PHYBGCWAV_DISCRETE_MYNRT_013_035` and `INSITU_GLO_PHYBGCWAV_DISCRETE_MYNRT_013_030`

> **The de-tided current standard names are correct here.** Live-verified: `uo_detided` → `eastward_…_assuming_no_tide`, `vo_detided` → `northward_…_assuming_no_tide`. This matters because **the [IBI product](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md) has these two swapped in its delivered files**, and the [Baltic PUM](../sweden/bal-mfc-nemo.md) has them swapped in documentation. Med is the clean case, which narrows the IBI defect to that product's own post-processing rather than a shared Copernicus-wide template.

Med users can combine `wo` with the tracer fields directly; IBI users cannot.

### Static fields
`cmems_mod_med_phy_anfc_4.2km_static`, in three `--ext--` variants: coordinates, bathymetry, and mean dynamic topography. **Note this dataset still carries the `_202311` version tag** while every time-varying dataset is at `_202511`.

## Relationship to other entries

- **Wave counterpart — with a caveat:** **[MEDWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/greece/medwam.md)** (`MEDSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_006_017`) takes hourly surface currents and sea level from this product. But MEDWAM is a **WAM Cycle 6** system run by **HCMR in Greece**, while this product contains its own internal **WAVEWATCH III v6.07**. They are different wave models on different domains. The MEDWAM entry describes itself as "a fully coupled wave component of the Mediterranean Forecasting System" — accurate as to the forcing relationship, but **it should not be read as the wave half of this product's coupled system**, and that entry is worth annotating to that effect.

- **Parent global system:** **[GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md)** supplies the Atlantic boundary — the same parent as [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md), though without IBI's grid-inheritance relationship.

- **A three-way circular dependency across the Turkish Straits.** The Dardanelles boundary here is forced by `BLKSEA_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_007_001` daily fields plus a monthly Marmara Sea climatology. Reading the [Black Sea PUM](./blk-phy.md) closes the loop:
  - **Med-Physics** (this entry) ← Black Sea product + Marmara climatology
  - **Black Sea** ← the U-TSS Marmara model
  - **U-TSS** ← **both** the Black Sea *and* Mediterranean MFC products

  So the Mediterranean feeds the Black Sea's Marmara sub-system, which feeds the Black Sea, which feeds the Mediterranean. **Both products are run by CMCC**, which is presumably how the loop is sequenced without deadlock, but **no PUM documents the ordering** (*TBD*).

  Note also that the Marmara climatology used here (Maderich et al. 2015) is a **different model** from the U-TSS output shipped inside the Black Sea product — the catalogue contains two non-equivalent Marmara representations.

- **Indirect dependency on EFAS.** River runoff comes from **EFAS v5**. The EFAS medium-range forecast is itself excluded from this repository because its public feed carries a 30-day embargo, so this is a case where an excluded product's output reaches users through an included one. Worth a line in [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md).

- **De-tided naming comparison:** this product is the **correct reference case** for de-tided current standard names. See the warning in [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md).

- **Which to use when:** for the Mediterranean basin this is the only Copernicus physics option. In the Atlantic box west of Gibraltar it overlaps [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md), which is finer horizontally (1/36° against 1/24°) but much coarser vertically (50 levels against 141) — a genuine trade-off for anyone working the Gibraltar exchange or Alboran Sea.

- **Programme context:** see [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md) for the full Copernicus Marine portfolio.

## Notes

- **The internal WW3 is not MEDWAM.** The single most consequential thing to get right about this product. See *Coupling*.

- **The wave coupling is undocumented beyond a version string.** The PUM names WW3 v6.07 and calls the system coupled but specifies no coupler, no coupling frequency, no exchanged fields, and no direction. Both the IBI and AMM15 PUMs document their wave coupling in detail; this one does not (**TBD**).

- **141 levels is exceptional.** No other Copernicus Marine regional physics product comes close — the Baltic has 56, IBI 50, AMM15 33, AMM7 24, TOPAZ5 40. Combined with z\* and partial cells, this is the best-resolved water column in the portfolio, and it is delivered on the **native** grid rather than interpolated to standard depths.

- **The assimilation is daily, not weekly, despite what the summary table implies.** The General Information table lists "Update frequency: Analysis Weekly", which describes the analysis *product* cadence. The production-cycle text is explicit that the assimilation cycle is daily (24 h) in filter mode in both the daily and weekly cycles. The weekly Tuesday cycle produces a *better* analysis over a longer look-back window (J−15 to J−1), not the only analysis.

- **Every day rewrites the previous three days.** The daily cycle delivers an adjusted analysis for J−3 to J−1 alongside the forecast, and the Tuesday cycle rewrites back to J−15. Anyone harvesting this product incrementally will find recent values changing under them for up to two weeks — this is by design, but it makes naive append-only archiving unsafe.

- **Vertical velocity shares the tracer grid.** Unlike [IBI](../spain/ibi-phy-005-001.md), `wo` here is on the same 141 levels as `thetao`/`so`/`uo`/`vo`, live-verified. No re-interpolation needed.

- **The 3D hourly datasets have a very short archive.** They begin 2025-10-06 against 2023-11-01 for everything else — roughly ten months against nearly three years. The 2-year rolling window does not yet apply to them.

- **The static dataset was not reversioned.** It carries `_202311` while all time-varying datasets are at `_202511`. Whether the static fields genuinely predate the current configuration or the tag simply was not bumped is unclear (**TBD**) — the same pattern seen on the [Baltic monthly dataset](../sweden/bal-mfc-nemo.md).

- **De-tided datasets are one day shorter**, ending 2026-09-01 against 2026-09-02 — the expected Doodson-filter cost, consistent with the Baltic and IBI products.

- **Daily means changed timestamp convention in December 2019**, moving from 00:00Z-centred to 12:00Z-centred. Outside the current 2-year window, but relevant to anyone using the multiyear reanalysis alongside this product.

- **River forcing is a live hydrological forecast, not a climatology.** EFAS v5 makes this the only Copernicus regional physics product in this repository with genuinely dynamic river input — AMM15, AMM7, and IBI all use climatologies to some degree. The Po was upgraded to daily observations separately in September 2021.

- **The Dardanelles boundary is half-climatological.** Monthly Marmara Sea climatology blended with daily Black Sea fields. For anyone studying Aegean–Black Sea exchange this is the limiting assumption in the setup.
