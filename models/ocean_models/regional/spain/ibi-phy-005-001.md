# IBI-V7 (Iberia-Biscay-Ireland Ocean Physics Analysis and Forecast)

## What this model is
The IBI Ocean Physics Analysis and Forecast is the operational regional ocean physics system of the Copernicus Marine **Iberia-Biscay-Ireland Monitoring and Forecasting Centre (IBI MFC)**, run by **NOW Systems** with supercomputing support from **CESGA** in Spain.

It is an eddy-resolving **NEMO v3.6** application at **1/36°** (~2–3 km) on a refined subset of the ORCA 1/12° tripolar grid, with 50 z-levels, explicit tides from FES2014, and a **reduced-order Kalman filter (SAM2/SEEK)** assimilation running a weekly cycle. Model code development is Mercator Ocean International's; the operational suite is Spanish.

Two features set it apart from the other Copernicus Marine regional physics systems:

- **Genuine two-way wave coupling.** The forecast run ingests Stokes drift, wave-modified surface stress, and wave-breaking TKE from [IBIWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/spain/ibiwam.md), which in turn takes surface currents from this system. Among the Copernicus regional physics products only this and [AMM15](../uk/amm15-nws.md) have a real bidirectional wave exchange.
- **The richest temporal offering in the portfolio** — 15-minute instantaneous surface fields, hourly surface means, hourly 3D means, daily and monthly 3D means, plus separate de-tided and vertical-velocity datasets. Fourteen datasets in total.

The official Copernicus Marine product identifier is `IBI_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_005_001`. The current operational system version is **IBI-V7** (since 26 November 2024).

> **This product also spent two years being redistributed under a different identifier.** Between September 2023 and November 2025 the Copernicus North West Shelf physics and wave products were operationally extractions of the IBI system. See *Relationship to other entries*.

*Verified 2026-08-25 from the PUM (Issue 8.3, June 2025), Marine Data Store STAC records, and live ARCO Zarr metadata.*

## What area it covers
- **Coverage:** Iberia-Biscay-Ireland Atlantic waters — western and northern Iberia, Bay of Biscay, French Atlantic coast, Irish and UK Atlantic approaches, the Gibraltar Strait and Alboran fringe, and the North African Atlantic coast south to ~26°N
- **Domain bounds (documented):** 19°W – 5°E, 26°N – 56°N
- **Domain bounds (live-verified):** **19.0828°W – 5.0846°E, 26.1654°N – 56.0829°N**
- **Grid dimensions (live-verified):** **871 × 1078** (longitude × latitude)
- **Grid spacing (live-verified):** 0.02777863° in both directions, i.e. 1/36°
- **Native grid:** 1/36° staggered Arakawa C-grid, a refined subset of the ORCA 1/12° tripolar grid. Delivered fields are **bilinearly interpolated** onto the regular lat-lon grid above.
- **Special masked or excluded regions:** land masked; open-boundary relaxation zones are 30 grid points wide, within which bathymetry is set to the parent global model's values and progressively merged inward

> **The PUM's grid figures do not match the delivered data.** It states the domain as [−19, 5] × [26, 56] and the grid as "1081 x 865" at 0.02778° spacing. Live data gives **871 longitude × 1078 latitude**, on a grid offset from the stated bounds — wider in longitude (24.167° against 24.000°) and slightly shorter in latitude (29.918° against 30.000°). The documented figures are exactly what those round bounds would produce, so they appear to be computed from the nominal domain rather than read from a file. **Note that the [IBIWAM entry](../../../wave_models/regional/spain/ibiwam.md) carries the same 1081 × 865 figure**, presumably from the wave PUM — worth re-verifying against live wave files.

## Basic details
- **Model type:** Regional eddy-resolving ocean physics; deterministic; wave-coupled; with data assimilation
- **Core ocean model:** **NEMO v3.6**
- **Sea ice model:** None (domain is ice-free)
- **System name:** IBI-V7 (operational since 26 November 2024)
- **Horizontal resolution:** 1/36° (~2–3 km)
- **Vertical levels:** **50 z-levels (geopotential)**, resolution decreasing from ~1 m in the upper 10 m to more than 400 m in the deep ocean. Partial-step representation of the bottom wet cell, constrained so the minimum bottom cell thickness exceeds 15 m or 20 % of the reference thickness.
- **Vertical grid (live-verified, T-points, m):** 0.494, 1.541, 2.646, 3.819, 5.078, 6.441, 7.930, 9.573, 11.405, 13.467 … 3992.484, 4405.224, 4833.291, 5274.784, **5727.917** — the same 50-level set used by the parent [GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md) system, which is deliberate (see *Forcing*)
- **Vertical grid (live-verified, W-points, m):** **a different 50-level set** — 0.000, 1.011, 2.086, 3.223, 4.437, 5.745, 7.168, 8.730, 10.463, 12.404 … 3792.474, 4196.816, 4617.462, 5052.474, **5500.001**. See *Notes*.
- **Free surface:** non-linear split-explicit (Shchepetkin and McWilliams 2004), with all vertical thicknesses remapped every timestep to accommodate large tidal sea-level excursions on the shelf
- **Vertical mixing:** k-ε in the generic form of Umlauf and Burchard (2003), including surface wave-breaking induced mixing; background turbulence modified for unresolved internal tides following de Lavergne et al. (2016)
- **Tracer advection:** QUICKEST (Leonard 1979) limited by Zalesak (1979)
- **Lateral mixing:** horizontal biharmonic operators for momentum and tracers
- **Forecast length:** **10 days**, plus a 1-day hindcast, updated daily. Raised from 5 days at the November 2024 upgrade.
- **Analysis:** weekly, run **Mondays and Wednesdays**, replacing the hindcast; delivered as historic IBI best estimates
- **Update frequency:** daily forecast; twice-weekly analysis
- **Target delivery time:** **14:00 UTC** for the 1-day hindcast plus 10-day forecast. Monthly files for month−1 appear on the **20th** of each month.
- **Temporal output resolution:** 15-minute instantaneous (surface), hourly mean (surface), hourly mean (3D), daily mean (25-hour averaged), monthly mean
- **Archive availability:** 2-year rolling — but live extents differ substantially by dataset, see *Data availability*
- **Bathymetry source:** **GEBCO 08** (30 arc-second, Becker et al. 2009), merged with local databases

## Forcing
- **Atmospheric forcing:** **hourly ECMWF** — 10 m wind, surface pressure, 2 m temperature, 2 m specific humidity, precipitation rate, short-wave and long-wave radiative fluxes. IFS empirical bulk formulae (ECMWF 2014; Brodeau et al. 2017) compute sensible and latent heat flux, evaporation, and surface stress.
- **Solar penetration:** five-band exponential scheme including UV, as a function of surface chlorophyll from the Copernicus Marine ESA-CCI North East Atlantic product at monthly frequency (`OCEANCOLOUR_ATL_CHL_L4_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_009_090` in NRT; the `..._REP_..._009_091` reprocessed product otherwise)
- **River runoff:** **33 point sources** for the main rivers, using daily PREVIMER observations (9 rivers), SMHI hydrological model data, or climatology from GRDC and the French *Banque Hydro* on a best-available basis. Since April 2017 an **additional monthly climatological coastal runoff rate** (from the Dai and Trenberth climatology) supplements the 33 rivers, chosen to make the IBI freshwater forcing consistent with the parent global system.
- **Lateral boundary conditions:** **[GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md)** (`GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024`), the Copernicus Marine global eddy-resolving system at 1/12°, supplying temperature, salinity, velocities, and sea level from daily outputs. The IBI vertical grid is **identical to the parent's**, so no vertical extrapolation of boundary data is needed.
- **Initial conditions:** also from GLO12
- **Tidal forcing:** **11 harmonics — M2, S2, N2, K1, O1, Q1, M4, K2, P1, Mf, Mm** — from the **FES2014** solution (Carrere et al. 2015), applied at the open boundaries, plus the **loading tide** (seabed deflection under the weight of tidal water masses) as a 2D forcing across the whole domain. The atmospheric pressure component missing from the parent's sea level is added under an inverse-barometer approximation.
- **Mediterranean boundary correction:** **+1 cm** added to the sea surface height imposed at the Mediterranean open boundary, tuned to bring the net Atlantic-to-Mediterranean flux near 0.05 Sv (Soto-Navarro et al. 2010; Adloff et al. 2015) and improve Alboran Sea circulation.
- **Wave forcing:** see *Coupling*

## Coupling
**Two-way exchange with the IBI wave system**, active in the forecast runs since the December 2020 release. Three wave-driven processes enter the ocean model:

- **Stokes–Coriolis** forcing, using Stokes velocity components from the wave system — generally the dominant wind-correlated surface drift and a source of upper-ocean mixing
- **Surface stress partition** — the wind stress left to the ocean after wave consumption, via the neutral drag coefficient and the τ_oc/τ_a ratio supplied by the wave system
- **Turbulent kinetic energy flux** released to the ocean, computed from the wave dissipation source term and parameterised following Craig and Banner (1994)

The wave fields ingested are Stokes velocity components, significant wave height, wind-sea wave height, drag coefficient, mean wave period, peak wave number, the Craig–Banner coefficient, the energy flux to ocean, and the momentum flux of surface stress to ocean — all from **[IBIWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/spain/ibiwam.md)** (`IBI_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_005_005`), which takes this system's surface currents in return.

> **Wave forcing applies to the forecast runs only.** The PUM's version history records the December 2020 addition as "Wave forcing (only forecast run)". The weekly analysis is therefore not wave-forced, which introduces a systematic difference between the analysis and forecast portions of a continuous series (**flagged; the PUM does not say whether this restriction still holds under IBI-V7**).

There is no atmospheric coupling — ECMWF forcing is one-way.

## Data assimilation
- **DA scheme:** **SAM2** (*Système d'Assimilation de Mercator* version 2), a **reduced-order Kalman filter** built on the **SEEK** formulation (Singular Evolutive Extended Kalman Filter; Pham et al. 1998) — the same family as the scheme used by the parent [GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md) system
- **Assimilation window:** **7 days**. An analysis is performed every day within the cycle, producing increments of sea surface height, temperature, salinity, and velocity.
- **Increment application:** **Incremental Analysis Update (IAU)** — increments applied progressively through the hindcast run of the same cycle, avoiding the shock that a direct update would induce. *(Contrast with [TOPAZ5](../norway/topaz5.md), which deliberately does not use IAU and shows a weekly discontinuity as a result.)*
- **Innovation timing:** First Guess at Appropriate Time (FGAT) — observation-minus-forecast computed during model integration at the correct location and time
- **Error covariance:** based on a collection of predefined 3D ocean state anomalies computed from a **7-year free-run experiment** relative to a running mean over a ±48-day window, for T, S, U, V, and SSH. The analysis increment is a linear combination of these anomalies. Conceptually close to Ensemble Optimal Interpolation (Oke et al. 2008).
- **Localisation:** covariances set to zero beyond twice the local spatial correlation scale; zonal, meridional, and temporal correlation scales define an "influence bubble" around each analysis point.
- **Observation operator:** bilinear remapping onto the four grid points surrounding each observation. For SLA assimilation the model sea level is filtered of its high-frequency barotropic signal, mirroring altimetric data processing.

### Assimilated observations
- **Sea level anomaly:** filtered along-track SLA from `SEALEVEL_EUR_PHY_L3_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_008_059`
- **In-situ T/S profiles:** `INSITU_GLO_TS_ASSIM_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_013_047` in near real time. The CORA 4.1 database (Cabanes et al. 2013) was used for the hindcast calibration run — note the General Information table still cites CORA 4.1 as the operational source, which the detailed text corrects.
- **Satellite SST:** near-real-time **ODYSSEA** L3S, `SST_GLO_SST_L3S_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_010_010`. *(The production-system summary table says "Satellite sea surface temperature HR (ODYSSEA)"; the General Information table says "OSTIA". The detailed text names ODYSSEA — see* Notes.*)*

> Live-verified 2026-08-25: **`uo_detided` carries `standard_name = northward_sea_water_velocity_assuming_no_tide`** and **`vo_detided` carries `eastward_sea_water_velocity_assuming_no_tide`**. The variable names follow the u = eastward, v = northward convention; the standard names contradict them.

Two different operators, the same error, one propagated into data and one not, suggests a shared de-tided post-processing template rather than independent mistakes. Both products' de-tided datasets were introduced in the same November 2024 upgrade cycle, as were the NWS ones during the IBI-extraction era. **Worth reporting to the Copernicus Marine service desk.**

## Relationship to other entries

- **Coupled counterpart:** **[IBIWAM](../../../wave_models/regional/spain/ibiwam.md)** (`IBI_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_005_005`) — genuinely bidirectional. This system supplies surface currents to the wave model; the wave model returns Stokes drift, stress partition, and TKE flux to the forecast runs. Same operator, same programme, same domain. Note the wave model runs at the same 1/36° resolution but on a **larger native grid** (20°W–17°E, 25°N–64.6°N) post-processed down to the IBI domain.

- **Parent global system:** **[GLO12](../../global/france/glo12.md)** (`GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024`) supplies both initial and lateral boundary conditions, at 1/12°. The relationship is unusually tight: the IBI horizontal grid is a **refined subset of the same ORCA tripolar grid**, the vertical grid is **identical**, boundary-zone bathymetry is set to the parent's values, and the freshwater forcing was deliberately tuned for consistency with the parent. Both systems also use the **same SAM2/SEEK assimilation family**. IBI is a genuine downscaling of GLO12 rather than an independent regional system.

- **Was redistributed as the NWS products, 2023–2025.** Between September 2023 and November 2025 the Copernicus North West Shelf **physics** (`NWSHELF_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_004_013`) and **wave** (`NWSHELF_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_004_014`) products were operationally **extractions of the IBI system** cropped to the NWS domain, before the UK Met Office re-introduced its own systems in November 2025. Users pulling NWS data in that window were receiving IBI output. See [AMM15 physics](../uk/amm15-nws.md) and [AMM15-WW3](../../../wave_models/regional/uk/amm15-ww3-uk.md) for the other side of both transitions, and note that both those products' current Copernicus archives still contain IBI-era data.

- **Overlapping regional coverage:** the IBI domain overlaps the southern NWS domain (Bay of Biscay, Celtic Sea, Irish Sea approaches, western English Channel). Since November 2025 these are two genuinely independent systems — different operators, different NEMO configurations, different DA schemes, different tidal sets — so the overlap now offers a real choice rather than a relabelling. IBI extends much further south (to 26°N, into Moroccan and Mauritanian Atlantic waters) than either NWS configuration.

- **Which to use when:** for the Iberian, Biscay, and North African Atlantic, this entry. For the shelf seas north and east of Brittany at higher resolution and with sub-hourly output, [AMM15](../uk/amm15-nws.md). For the wider North-East Atlantic at coarser resolution, [AMM7](../uk/amm7-nws-lr.md). In the geographic overlap, IBI offers 50 levels against AMM15's 33 and a wave-coupled rather than wave-coupled-plus-BGC configuration.

- **Programme context:** see [`COPERNICUS.md`](../../../../COPERNICUS.md) for the full Copernicus Marine portfolio.

## Notes

- **The de-tided current standard names are swapped in the data.** See the warning under *What it provides*. This is the most consequential thing to know about this product.

- **Vertical velocity is on a different vertical grid from every other 3D field.** `wo` is delivered on **W-points** (cell interfaces), running 0.000 → 5500.001 m, while `thetao`, `so`, `uo`, and `vo` are on **T-points**, running 0.494 → 5727.917 m. Both are labelled `elevation` with 50 values and neither dataset flags the difference. Combining `wo` with the tracer fields without re-interpolation will misalign them by roughly half a cell — increasing with depth, where the offset reaches ~228 m at the deepest level.

- **The PUM's grid dimensions and domain bounds are nominal, not actual.** See *What area it covers*. The same figures appear in the IBIWAM entry and should be re-checked there.

- **The assimilated SST product is named three ways.** The General Information table says **OSTIA**; the production-system summary table says **"Satellite sea surface temperature HR (ODYSSEA)"**; the detailed assimilation text names **ODYSSEA** with the specific product identifier `SST_GLO_SST_L3S_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_010_010`. The detailed text is the most specific and is taken as authoritative here, but the General Information table's OSTIA claim is unexplained (**flagged, not resolved**).

- **The assimilated in-situ profile source is also stated two ways.** The General Information table cites the **CORA 4.1** database; the detailed text says CORA 4.1 was used for the hindcast calibration run and that `INSITU_GLO_TS_ASSIM_NRT_OBSERVATIONS_013_047` is what is assimilated operationally. The summary table appears not to have been updated.

- **Wave forcing may still be forecast-only.** The December 2020 change log entry specifies "only forecast run", and nothing in the PUM revokes it. If the weekly analysis remains wave-free, the analysis and forecast portions of a continuous series are dynamically inconsistent in the upper ocean (**TBD**).

- **The 3D hourly dataset has a much shorter archive than the rest.** It carries a separate version tag (`_202506`) and begins 2024-11-10, against 2022-11 for the daily, 15-minute, and de-tided datasets. The June 2025 release notes record its time range being extended, so it is being backfilled — but it does not yet reach the nominal 2-year window.

- **De-tided datasets end one day earlier than the others.** Live-verified: 2026-09-01 against 2026-09-02, the expected cost of the Doodson 39-hour centred filter. The same behaviour is documented for the [Baltic product](../sweden/bal-mfc-nemo.md).

- **The 15-minute dataset changed meaning in November 2022**, from averaged to instantaneous values. Anyone using that dataset across the boundary is splicing two different quantities.

- **A ~3 cm SSH discontinuity exists at 19 November 2016** in the long archive, caused by an upgrade of the parent global system. Documented in the PUM's own version table.

- **A +1 cm SSH correction is applied at the Mediterranean boundary** as a deliberate tuning to reach a realistic Atlantic-to-Mediterranean net flux. Users comparing IBI sea level against tide gauges near Gibraltar should be aware this is not a purely prognostic field there.
